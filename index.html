      Weather Window

      The circle is the average. The lines are the week. The forecast tells you what might happen; Weather Window helps you understand what the week could feel like.

      Data. Forecast and geocoding by Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Current conditions are model estimates, not station observations. Free API for non-commercial use. The lens is an illustration of these values, not a simulation.

      UV. Categories and advice follow the WHO UV index guidance.

      Privacy. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. Settings and saved places stay in this browser. City searches and coordinates (rounded to ~1 km) go to Open-Meteo only.

      Your data in Weather Window

      • Stored in this browser only: units, theme, your planner conditions, saved places, the last place you viewed, and up to four recent forecasts so the page can open instantly.
      • Sent to Open-Meteo: the text you search for, and the coordinates of the place you view (rounded to 0.01°, about 1 km).
      • Never collected: accounts, analytics, cookies, or tracking. Nothing is synced between devices.
